Student team measures water use in campus gardens

A student research team installed sensors in three campus gardens to measure soil moisture and water consumption. The experiment compares scheduled irrigation with a system that waters plants only when the soil becomes dry. Measurements will continue for six weeks.

Students will record temperature and rainfall each day. They will compare plant growth and total water use across the gardens. The team expects the sensor system to reduce unnecessary watering while keeping plants healthy.

The final report will include the raw measurements, a description of the equipment, and recommendations for future experiments. The data will be available to other students for classroom analysis.
