Campus library extends evening study hours

The university library will remain open until ten in the evening during the examination period. Students can use the quiet reading rooms, borrow textbooks, and access the digital research collection. The new schedule starts on Sunday and continues for four weeks.

Library staff will help students locate journal articles and reserve group study rooms. Students must bring their university identification cards to enter after six in the evening. Food is not allowed in the reading rooms, but water bottles are welcome.

The library team will review attendance at the end of the examination period before deciding whether to keep the extended schedule next semester.
