New campus shuttle route connects student housing

The university transport office is introducing a shuttle route between student housing and the engineering building. Buses will leave every fifteen minutes from seven in the morning until nine in the evening. The service is free for students and university employees.

The route includes stops at the main gate, the medical center, and the sports hall. Passengers should arrive five minutes before departure. Each bus has space for wheelchairs and a display showing the next stop.

The transport office will collect passenger feedback during the first month. It will use the results to adjust departure times and improve connections with existing routes.
